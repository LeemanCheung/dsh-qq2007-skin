# Notices

`dsh-qq2007-skin` is an independent, unofficial visual plugin for DeepSeek Harness. It is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by Tencent, QQ, DeepSeek, or their respective owners.

“QQ” is used only to identify the historical interface era that inspired the project. Product names, logos, mascots, historical interface artwork, and related marks belong to their respective owners.

The plugin does **not** package QQ logos, Tencent artwork, historical QQ icons, historical sounds, audio files, binaries, or theme files. The pixel buddy in `assets/retro-buddy.svg`, the preview, and the four raster artworks under `assets/generated-source/` are original project assets distributed under the repository's MIT license. The optional send chime is synthesized at runtime from two short sine-wave oscillators and was not derived from a QQ sound. The raster artworks were created with Codex's native `gpt-image-2` image-generation capability from the committed prompts; no historical screenshot or third-party image was used as model input.

Design and feature research referenced the author's MIT-licensed [Codex-QQ2007-Skin](https://github.com/LeemanCheung/Codex-QQ2007-Skin), but this repository uses DSH's native Cordis client-plugin and ThemeRuntime extension points rather than its CDP injection mechanism.
